## Track maintenance

Limestone [tracks](/guide/bike-tracks) built 75 to 100mm deep mostly need an occasional weed spray and some attention around the edges of the riding track. Pump and skills tracks need weeding too, and a limestone pump track might need a light top-up once a year.

Most schools budget around $3,000 a year for upkeep (see [Costs and funding](/guide/costs-and-funding)) and fold track work into regular property maintenance, handled by the caretaker. An annual working bee, or a call back to whoever built the track, works just as well. A levelling bar makes building and maintaining a limestone riding track much easier, and a power broom is great for tidying up the skills track.

## Bike maintenance

Keep the fleet running with a simple routine: tyres pumped, chains oiled, brakes checked before every ride, nuts and bolts tightened regularly. Some schools train up a few student "bike monitors" to handle these checks day-to-day.

- Buy good-quality [bikes](/guide/bikes)
- Keep them out of the rain
- Store them securely every night in your [bike storage](/guide/bike-storage)
- Keep tyres pumped
- Oil chains regularly
- Check brakes before every ride
- Check nuts and bolts stay tight
- Get a bike mechanic to check the whole fleet at least once a year

For further reading: [Bike Maintenance Schedule (PDF)](/downloads/bike-maintenance-schedule.pdf). For video walkthroughs, Auckland Transport's [Bike Maintenance 101 playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwdQL7ny3E69KmXMU7crT2jPIvfPMqxE5) is a good one to share with staff.

## Check your bike: Air, Brakes, Chain

- **A: Air.** Are the tyres properly pumped up?
- **B: Brakes.** Do the front and back brakes both work?
- **C: Chain.** Is it oiled and moving freely?

[More on checking your bike](https://www.bikeready.govt.nz/adults/tips-for-everyday-bike-riding-beginners-to-advanced/how-to-check-your-bike/) from BikeReady.
